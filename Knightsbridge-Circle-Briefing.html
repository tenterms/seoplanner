Knightsbridge Circle vs Sienna Charles

The client thinks it is being outranked. The data says it is winning the UK and catching up fast in the US, but Sienna Charles still owns the generic American head terms, gets cited twice as often by AI assistants, and has a LinkedIn audience six times the size. That gap is the pitch.

Verdict

"Outranked" is half true and the half that is true is fixable. In the UK, Sienna Charles is invisible (8 keywords, 35 visits a month) and KC holds position 5 on both London commercial terms. In the US, SC's three service pages own the generic head terms (luxury concierge service, luxury lifestyle concierge, luxury travel concierge, vip concierge) while KC's city pages have quietly taken New York, Los Angeles and Miami. KC has no page targeting any generic head term, its travel page ranks for nothing, and its About page is a 404. The real gaps are structural: entity content for AI answers, the generic service layer, citable third-party assets, and audience on LinkedIn.

Sienna Charles is also more fragile than it looks: two years of gently declining traffic, 40% of it from restaurant reviews in its magazine, a founder who is the brand, and an employee-review problem that shows on its own name search. KC's momentum, transparent pricing and press bench are the assets to build on.

07 · How each site is built

SC 60-ish URLs, four layers

  • Service layer (3 pages): vip-luxury-concierge, luxury-lifestyle-concierge, luxury-travel-concierge. These hold every head term.
  • Conversion layer: membership, become-a-member, about/our-founder, press, contact.
  • Citable layer: Voyage magazine (wine, fine dining, interiors, beauty, wellness, yachting), destination pages (luxury-hotels/miami, michelin-star-restaurants/london, private-luxury-villas/sicily, private-island-rental/bahamas).
  • Product layer: experiences.siennacharles.com (Antarctica, Bordeaux, Paris Olympics, Play Augusta, F1 Vegas Paddock, Jiro privatised, Bhutan by helicopter).
  • Behind Cloudflare, so crawlers (ours included) get a 403; robots blocks /blog. No city pages.

KC 33 URLs, two layers and a gap

  • City layer (11): London, New York, LA, Miami, Dallas, Palm Beach, Boston, Dubai, Singapore, Amsterdam, Lisbon. 520–730 words each, no FAQ, no LocalBusiness or Service schema, only nav links pointing in, no cross-links between cities or to venues.
  • Access layer (9): venue pages with a "we are not the venue" disclaimer and no link back to the city page or membership.
  • Missing: the generic service layer (lifestyle, VIP, corporate, family office), About and founder pages (/about is a 404), press, reviews, comparison, membership explainer. One blog post (pricing).
  • Technical: clean 301s from the old flat URLs; London meta description is broken ("Tel: +44 (0) 203 6275 769Find us on Google"); travel page has no meta description; GTM and Crisp chat only, no CRM pixel, no LinkedIn Insight tag, no conversion event on the application form.

14 · Risks and open items
  • Consolidation in progress. The July URL move is still settling; August traffic dipped 30%. Any further slug changes, redesigns or redirects reset the clock. Content, links and schema only.
  • Sienna Charles is a US brand with a US founder-celebrity. Parity on brand search (600 vs 70) is a multi-year PR job, not an SEO one. Pitch the winnable layer (head terms, AI ingredients, city dominance), not brand-volume parity.
  • AI Overview text could not be captured for the head terms (Google returned empty overviews to the follow-up call). Ahrefs Brand Radar is not on the plan. Run the prompt panel by hand before the meeting so the AI slide carries live examples.
  • Sienna Charles is Cloudflare-protected, so page-level on-page findings for SC come from Ahrefs and Google rather than a crawl.
  • Capacity. Founder time for LinkedIn and entity content is the constraint most likely to stall delivery. Scope it explicitly.
  • Reputation landmine on their side, not ours: SC's name search surfaces Glassdoor, Indeed and Reddit employee complaints and a "lawsuit" related search. Useful context; do not use it in the pitch.
