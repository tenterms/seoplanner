# BNI Premier Southampton: Referral Target Report

Prepared for Joe Shervell (TenTerms), 28 August 2026.

The brief: find strong TenTerms ICP targets that are **provably linked** to members of BNI Premier Southampton, so weekly referral asks can name a specific company and the member who holds the path to it.

## How it was built

1. **Roster** pulled from the chapter's public member list: 19 members (18 excluding TenTerms).
2. **Evidence harvest** across every member's website: named clients, case studies, testimonials with company attribution, partner pages, and charity sponsor lists. Cross-checked against the Ahrefs link graph (outbound portfolio links, footer credits).
3. **ICP screen**: candidates checked against Companies House (age, status, SIC) and an Ahrefs visibility snapshot (GB organic traffic, keyword counts, domain rating). The TenTerms sweet spot is a good established business whose organic visibility is far below the quality of the business.

Evidence rows (52) with URLs are in `evidence-log.csv`. Strength codes: **A** = documented client relationship (portfolio, case study, named testimonial, client wall); **B** = sponsor or association; **C** = link-only or franchise-generic, verify before using.

## Caveats

- Companies House lookups used name matching; the flagged ambiguities (Searle & Co liquidation entity, Actons dormant namesake, Hellards, Chicken & Blues branch entities) need verification before any approach.
- Ahrefs traffic figures are estimates; treat as order-of-magnitude.
- Evidence proves a *relationship existed when published*; sites can be stale (one harvested domain, allingtonnursery.com, is now a hijacked expired domain). Confirm currency with the member — which is exactly what the 1-2-1 is for.
- ActionCOACH testimonials are franchise-wide and not attributed to Kevin specifically; not usable as his connections without asking him.
