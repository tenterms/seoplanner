## 3. What they do and who they sell to

Training-led fire safety compliance: 13 service pages covering fire safety awareness, fire marshal, extinguisher and evacuation chair training (with dedicated "for Carers" variants), responsible person and fire door awareness courses, fire risk assessments, fire door inspections, evacuation drills, FEEPs/PEEPs and policy work, plus digital fire simulator hire (a genuine differentiator locally). E-learning is priced on the site (£15–£65); all in-person work is quoted. They do not service extinguishers or install alarms.

Positioning: "our team consists entirely of former Fire Officers with over 30 years' practical experience". Strong care-sector specialisation: most of the 16 named case studies are care and rest homes, alongside the Maritime & Coastguard Agency (repeat fire warden training), schools and charities. Search strategy implication: their money terms are local commercial ("fire risk assessment Southampton", "fire warden training Southampton") and the care-home niche is their content moat.

## 6. Search and AI visibility snapshot

*A spot check from public searches and Ahrefs, not a rank-tracked audit.*

**Commercial terms that matter**: fire risk assessment Southampton / Hampshire; fire warden (marshal) training Southampton; fire safety training Hampshire; fire door inspection Hampshire; care home fire safety training.

**Where they appear**: one bright spot — **#3 organic and #1 in the local pack (4.9★, 15 reviews) for "fire safety training Hampshire"**. Everywhere else, absent: not in the top 10 organic or the local pack for "fire risk assessment Southampton" (their flagship assessment service) or "fire warden training Southampton" (a core course).

**Trend**: organic traffic has grown from ~0 (late 2023) to ~170 visits/month (Sep 2026) — but it is almost entirely informational blog traffic ("how often should fire drills be carried out", "what is a FEEP", an evacuation-chair guide ranking #1 nationally). The blog is compounding nicely and capturing nothing commercial. Classic blog-strong/commerce-weak: the demand lands on guides, and the service pages that convert are invisible.

**Competitor set buyers actually see**: HCS Safety (Southampton, 4.9★ ×52), EchoFour Training (based at Southampton Airport, run by *current serving firefighters* — a direct counter to Benfield's ex-fire-officer USP, staffed from the same airport fire service Benfield's own team came from), Fire Aid Academy (4.8★ ×44), IPS Fire & Security, CE Safety, Pyro Fire (owns "fire risk assessment Southampton"), Arundel Fire & Security, plus £18–£30 online course players squeezing training queries.

**AI spot check**: no AI answer box cites Benfield on any query checked; the "best X" slots go to Checkatrade/listicle content. Their AI-visibility ingredients are half-there (strong accreditations and named case studies; weak review volume, no comparison content, split domain identity).

**What a buyer experiences**: a care-home manager in Eastleigh searching "fire risk assessment Southampton" sees Pyro Fire, a Checkatrade list, the council, and three map-pack firms — and no trace of Benfield, whose office is fifteen minutes away and whose team assessed the Maritime & Coastguard Agency.

**On-site**: WordPress/Elementor, 13 templated service pages (adequate, not thin), 37-post active blog, but visible defects — machine-suggested title tags shipped unedited ("…Certified Experts in 25"), duplicated footer menus, a `{vendor_count}` placeholder, no company number on the site, and the benfieldfire/benfieldfs domain split.

## 7. What a new customer is worth to them

No published in-person pricing; sector norms: an in-person group course £300–£500; an FRA £300–£900 by premises size. A typical retained account (care home or SME: annual training refreshers, drills, periodic FRA) plausibly £1,000–£3,000 a year with strong retention — compliance renews on a cycle (the MCA case study is explicitly a 3-yearly repeat).

| Scenario | Extra enquiries/mo | Close rate | New customers/yr | First-12-month revenue impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 2 | 1 in 5 | ~5 | ~£7,500 (at £1,500 avg) |
| Realistic | 4 | 1 in 4 | ~12 | ~£24,000 (at £2,000 avg) |
| Stretch | 6 | 1 in 3 | ~24 | ~£60,000 (at £2,500 avg) |

(Retention upside is real — compliance clients renew for years — but the headline uses first-year value.)

**The conservative case does not clear 3× even the smallest TenTerms retainer (£18k/year).** The customer value is too low for the standard offer at Benfield's scale. Any engagement here is a small project or a group-level conversation.

## 8. Size of the prize

Standalone Benfield: modest — realistic case ~£24k/year of new revenue from local commercial visibility, which supports a one-off project (fix the money pages, reviews engine, local pack) but not a retainer.

Group-level Artius: unquantified but structurally larger — three brands, two regions, hardware + training cross-sell, and Benfield's compounding content asset. There is also a **migration risk that doubles as the opening**: if Artius folds benfieldfire.co.uk into the group's own site carelessly, they destroy the only organic asset they just bought. "Protect the traffic you just acquired" is a credible, time-sensitive first conversation with a new owner.

## 9. TenTerms fit and suggested entry point

**Do not pitch a retainer to Benfield.** Honest qualification: fails on size, ability to pay, and decision reachability in its current state.

Suggested play, in order:
1. **Park and verify** via a Donna 1-2-1: what she knows about the acquisition, whether Jason is staying, who now buys marketing. Costs nothing, and her own client relationship is exposed to the same change.
2. If there is appetite: a **group-level approach to Artius** (Paul Abbott) framed as a post-acquisition visibility review across the three brands, with the Benfield migration-protection angle as the hook. That is a paid review, not a retainer pitch.
3. Failing that, a **small tactical project** (£1k–£3k: retarget the money pages, title-tag cleanup, reviews generation) offered once the dust settles, positioned jointly with Donna.
