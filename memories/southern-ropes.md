## Validated verdicts (don't re-litigate)
- **mooring rope ≠ marine rope** → separate pages (Ropes Direct wins both with different collections). NEW /category/mooring-rope/.
- **dyneema = hmpe = uhmwpe** → ONE page (/material/hmpe/, retitled Dyneema-equivalent). Manufacturers (Marlow, English Braids) rank → winnable.
- **static = kernmantle = LSK** → owned by /category/low-stretch-kernmantle-rope/.
- **"rope splicing" bare = informational** (Marlow-style guide wins); service demand via "near me/service" → NEW /rope-splicing-service/ (GSC clicks at pos 2.9 with no page).
- **Do not chase:** "fire rope" (stove seals), bare "rope access"+cities (service industry), bare "tow rope" (car boot), "rope splicing jobs", crossword/pub homonyms.
- **cricket boundary rope** (150/mo KD0, seasonal Mar–Jul) winnable product page — client 2026 target; build by Feb.
- Open question for client: bell ropes ("bell ropes suppliers uk" clicks at pos 3.1 — do they make them?).
