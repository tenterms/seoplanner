## Validated verdicts (don't re-litigate)
- **CLIENT POSITIONING: business print only — NO funeral/wedding work.** Order of service printing (1,000/mo KD0 + funeral 600 KD1 + wedding 200 KD0, SERP winnable) was found, presented, and deliberately EXCLUDED at the client's direction (2026-07-30 revision). Documented in the workbook as an exclusion; do not re-pitch it. Consumer-photo intents (photo posters/postcards) also excluded. Church WEEKLY service sheets (order of service church 30/mo) stay in scope as an organisational purchase.
- **Bare national print terms (booklet/business card/sticker printing) = Vistaprint/instantprint/Mixam territory** — target via size/format long-tail (A5/stapled/spot-UV/folded, mostly KD0–3), Southampton local terms, and niches (programmes, parish magazines).
- **Services localise, sectors are national.** Local pack shows for printing southampton (100–150/mo), poster printing southampton (90), t-shirt printing southampton (200). GBP claim is the fastest local win. No per-town doorway pages.
- **Sector demand phrases as "[x] printing" / "printing for schools"**, not "printing for [sector]" mostly. Real sectors: schools/education ~730/mo (printing for schools 200 KD0 + printers for schools 200 KD0 + training manual 150 KD0), charities ~230, hospitality ~190 + menu cluster 1,700, photographers/artists ~260, theatres/events ~130, estate agents ~70. **Confirmed zero**: museums, cleaning, automotive, flooring, first aid, tutors, musicians, builders (but heras/scaffold/correx banner terms 150–200 each ride the PVC banner page). Client's hunch about the military-museum post was right.
- **Spot UV business cards 400/mo KD0, bookmark printing 400 KD0, compliment slips 450 KD0, magazine printing 800 KD1, foamex 500 KD0, qr code stickers 600 KD0** — cheap wins.
- **Evangelism site**: free bible uk 600 KD0 winnable top-5 (chapel pages win the SERP); flagged the free-Bible.co.uk domain overlap — ONE canonical home for the offer. GB church-commercial volumes tiny (gospel tracts 30, christian printing ~0); US sample big (gospel tracts 1,300, christian posters 900 KD0). Model: free-resource landing pages ← 198 posts feed them → print-services page converts. Division of labour: main site = commercial church print (banners 150 KD0, parish magazines 40, OOS), evangelism site = free resources + ministry.
- Only URL changes permitted: merge duplicate reviews pages, 2× magnet posts, 2× QR posts (single 301s). Everything else titles/H1s/meta/links only.
