# Project memory: Books Set In [Location] programmatic site (own venture)

**Client:** Internal — Joe's own programmatic content venture, informed by the prefixlookup.com BCBS experiment (20k pages → Google deindexed to 1; Bing 7k sessions/wk; ChatGPT 45+/wk).
**Deliverable:** `Books-Set-In-Location-Keyword-Universe-Site-Plan.xlsx` (this repo, 2026-08-28). 465 US + 250 GB keywords, 245 locations tiered P1(84)/P2(87)/P3(74), 43 non-geo settings. ~21k Ahrefs units.

## Validated verdicts (don't re-litigate)
- **World cities have ~no Google demand.** Probed 40: tokyo 10/mo US (30 global), berlin/cairo/beijing/sydney/seoul/moscow ≈ 0. Demand lives in **countries (italy 250, ireland 250), US states (maine 200, alaska 150), UK regions, + a short list of literary cities** (paris/london/new orleans 150, nyc 100, barcelona 70, rome/edinburgh 50-90 GB). Launch order: countries > US states > UK regions > literary cities; world cities are P3 AI-citation/map-completeness pages only.
- **KD 0-1 across the whole universe**; SERPs won by Goodreads UGC shelves, personal blogs, Reddit, Facebook groups → content-quality barrier is nil, authority/trust is the barrier (irrelevant on Bing).
- **tripfiction.com (the concept incumbent, database format): flat ~400-1,500/mo Google for 4.5 years.** Database-row pages don't rank — the page-level version of the BCBS lesson.
- **awellreadwanderer.com (curated blog): 11k/mo Google — but ~80% from literary-travel adjacencies** (Audible/Kindle reviews, Harry Potter London, bookshops-in-city, author landmarks); its "books set in Scotland" list itself earns ~69/mo. Adjacency editorial is the Google engine; location pages are the AI-citable dataset.
- **AI Overviews already on most head SERPs** (even tokyo/venice/amsterdam) → Google CTR structurally depressed; prize is being the cited source (Book+Place schema, per-book "why this place" sentences, lat/long mapping = the unique citable unit).
- **Total Google TAM is modest**: 16.2k/mo US + 5.1k/mo GB tracked volume (global ≈ 2× US on country terms). 1,000 sessions/wk target must come from Bing + AI + direct, per the brief's own thesis.
- **One page per intent**: novels/based-in/take-place/best/genre variants all parent-topic-confirmed to one page; genre = on-page anchor filters. new york = nyc = new york city (one page). georgia (US state) ≠ georgia (country).
- **Phase-2 axes validated**: non-geo settings (winter 200 US!, autumn, 1920s, ww2, medieval, ancient rome/greece/egypt, college, castles) ≈ doubles universe on same template; **author × place** mini-cluster real (harlan coben england question 80 US + 60 GB, stephen king maine 30, agatha christie devon, jane austen bath).
- URL pattern frozen at plan time: `/books-set-in/{slug}/` everything; `/explore/`; `/blog/…`. Never migrate.
