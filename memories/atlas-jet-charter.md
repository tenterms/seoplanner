## Key positioning verdicts
- Lead motivators: Security(trust/safety) > Time > Status > Comfort; Money only for membership (caps, banked savings, empty legs 20–70% off).
- Two buyer modes: occasional on-demand vs frequent flyer (10–25+ hr/yr) membership. EAs/family offices/concierges are a distinct gatekeeper audience with reputational-risk framing.
- Geographic edge: Far East / Australia / C&S America (Costa Rica #2 in Americas) — most brokers weak there. No Russia/Ukraine/war zones.
- Naming mismatch to resolve: questionnaire "Light jet card / Turbo card" vs site "Light Jets Card / All Aircraft Jet Card".
