## Core validated decisions (don't re-litigate)
- **"asic design services" = "asic design company"** — one page (4+ shared top-10 URLs: AnySilicon, ASIC North, Synopsys).
- **Bare "asic design" (1,300/mo US) is a glossary/Reddit/course SERP** — guide territory only; the service page must not chase it. Same for "asic chip" (1,200, TP 5,400) and "what is asic" (GB KD 59, contaminated by the Australian regulator).
- **One IC/semiconductor page, not four** — "chip design services" parent topic IS "semiconductor design services"; ic/vlsi/soc variants are H2s.
- **"electronic design services" = "electronics design services"** (same parent topic) — one hub page.
- **UK "asic design services" SERP is weak/winnable** (Ahrefs GB cache was empty → SERPAPI live): #1-2 a South African firm literally *named* "ASIC Design Services", then a directory, Cadence, Medium; Swindon Silicon the only real UK competitor.
- **Money signals:** fpga design services $9.00 CPC (best in dataset), electronics design services $11.00 US, chip design services $9.00, asic design flow $6.00 GB, electronic product development KD 0/$6.00 GB with paid ads.
- **UK winning format for umbrella terms** = service page with educational stages/process content (ByteSnap/Ignys pattern).
- **Verticals** (medical/industrial/IoT/RF/auto/aero, US 20-50/mo each) = sections on the ASIC page until case studies justify pages.
