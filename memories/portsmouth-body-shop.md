# Project memory: Portsmouth body shop (new venture, no site yet)

**Client:** Car body shop, Portsmouth UK (via Joe, 2026-07-30). From-scratch plan — no domain, no GSC, no rankings. Consumer body-repair shop window, but the value is insurance jobs (~10x ticket vs retail). Recovery service exists; footprint Portsmouth → Hampshire "within reason".
**Deliverable:** `Portsmouth-Body-Shop-SEO-Plan.xlsx` (this repo). 348-keyword GB universe, 15 clusters, 25-URL architecture, 3-route insurance playbook. UK only — no US sample (local intent).
**Scoping answers were never given** — defaults used: all 3 insurance routes mapped, Portsmouth+satellites geography, core+SMART+recovery scope, Excel deliverable. Revisit if client's actual positioning (approvals held? courtesy cars? claims partner?) contradicts.

## Market facts (Ahrefs GB, Jul 2026)
- National demand shape: car scratch repair 4,300 · alloy refurb 4,000 · car body repair near me 4,000 · car paint repair 2,900 · smart repair 1,800 · dents ~2,100 · bumpers ~2,000. "Near me" variants dominate → GBP/map pack carries most local demand.
- Measured Portsmouth: car body shop portsmouth 100 (KD34) · car body repair portsmouth 70 (KD11) · body shop portsmouth 60 (KD0) · alloy refurb 60 · car recovery 60 · tail of 10–30s. Bumper/respray/smart/accident + portsmouth = below Ahrefs floor.
- Satellites thin: fareham 40, gosport 10, chichester 60, havant/waterlooville 0. Southampton is a separate market (body shop southampton 200) — NOT targeted.
- Estimated total local addressable: ~200–350 searches/mo + GBP discovery (order-of-magnitude, ~0.8% UK pop method).

## Competition verdict
Portsmouth organic SERP is weak: DR 0–6 independents hold page 1 (Colourtech DR4 at #2–3, Stuarts/Mesa/A&B DR0). Aggregators: Checkatrade #3–4 (join it), Fix Auto DR60, ChipsAway DR49. Facebook/Reddit recommendation threads on page 1 = reputation market. Local pack incumbents: Pro Auto Body Repair, Colourtech, Scope, R&J. 6–12 months to page-1 on head terms is realistic.

## Validated verdicts (don't re-litigate)
- **The naming play:** most "accident repair centre" volume nationally is [brand]+ARC navigational (local proof: "hendy accident repair centre" 60/mo). Recommended trading identity includes "Accident Repair Centre".
- **Non-fault is THE wedge:** ~1,700/mo cluster at KD 0–3 with $4–11 CPCs ("non fault accident claim" $11). Commercial page + what-to-do guide. "will a non-fault accident affect my insurance uk" 350/mo KD0 — winnable from a new domain.
- **Right to choose your repairer = zero search volume.** Conversion messaging block on every insurance page, never a standalone SEO page.
- **Don't fight:** national "accident management company" (700, KD38, $9 CPC — funded nationals) · ~9k/mo courtesy-car policy-intent cluster · Southampton · windscreens (100/mo local but not the trade — referral).
- **Panel beater / bodywork / crash repair = homepage synonyms** — one page, never split.
- **Bundle "bumper respray cost" (150/mo) on the bumper service page** (mixed SERP); scratch/dent/respray cost questions get standalone national guides.
- Service slugs carry NO locality (titles/H1s do) so area growth never forces URL migration.

## Architecture shipped
Phase 1: home + /insurance/ silo (hub with right-to-choose section, /accident-repair-centre/, /non-fault-accident-repair/) + scratch/dent/bumper/respray/alloy/smart/recovery services + non-fault guide + claim-process & scratch-cost guides. Phase 2: Gosport/Fareham/Havant/Waterlooville location pages (substance-or-skip rule). Phase 3: Chichester, fleet.
Insurance routes: (1) insurer-approved — needs BS10125, small kw prize, big trust lift; (2) right-to-choose — messaging only; (3) non-fault — priority #1.
